## What exists today

**The decision this change carries.** The maintainer decided on 2026-11-05: "Appetite counts working time only, without verify-lock waits (bead coding-harness-po0a, 'Nur Arbeitszeit'); report an overrun when it happens" (`docs/journal/handover/2026-11-05-seven-lanes.md`, section "Decisions and preferences"). The trigger is recorded twice: lanes took 6 to 13 hours against 2 to 4 planned, mostly waiting for the verify lock (`plans/changes/adr-repair-check/decisions.md`, the Q&A recording of that session), and the waits against 2–4-hour appetites are mostly verify-lock wait (`docs/research/2026-11-07-night-run-plan.md`, lane plan). "Wait time outside appetite" stands as loose P2/P3 work in the long-term make-verify table (`docs/research/2026-11-09-make-verify-long-term.md`). The bead itself lives in the tracker database; this pass read no tracker state, so the bead's own notes are an unread source.

**The appetite rule (the stop).** `prompts/04-implement.md`, section "The appetite is a stop": the plan card names one `Appetite:`; *time used* is "the sum of the spans of the sessions that worked under this prompt on this change"; `date` is read at session start, resumption, between steps, before each commit and at the end, never estimated; a break inside a session counts and the status message says so; the time between two sessions does not; a stop ends the span and resuming work starts a new one. Each span is recorded with `python3 scripts/harness.py time-log <change> --start … --end … --seconds <n> --verb session --attempt <n> --route orchestrator --model <model> --result <accepted|rejected>`; a session under the Stage-2 runner never calls it — the runner logs its attempts itself. The first stop is a checkpoint: the session extends the appetite by itself once, up to twice the card's appetite (maintainer, 2026-10-30, also restated in `docs/WORKFLOW.md` §4 around the "An appetite that runs out" passage), and asks only at twice the appetite, at an open `[DECISION]`, or at a structural choice; at night the night rules decide instead. The card's appetite line is defined in `prompts/03-plan.md` ("Every `plan.md` opens with a card": `Appetite: <time for phase 4; a stop, not an estimate>`), and `templates/spec.md` carries the header field `appetite: <hours>  # hours of phase 4, repair rounds included — a stop, not an estimate`.

**The one time-record grammar.** `scripts/time_records.py` is the single writer, parser and inserter of the fixed records under a plan's `## Time used` heading (module docstring): the runner's attempts and the interactive session's spans share one line format so the run report counts both without interpreting prose (bead coding-harness-v6vk). The `Record` dataclass carries exactly `seconds, verb, attempt, route, model, result` plus the optional runner `scope` field (`format_entry` docstring); every record carries explicit whole seconds and durations are never derived from the UTC endpoints. `MARKER = "measured by the runner"`. `scripts/harness.py` implements the `time-log` verb (module docstring: "Two verbs: `decide adr` and `time-log`"), where `--route orchestrator` marks an interactive span. The runner side: `scripts/stage2.py` measures each completed attempt with `monotonic_now()` (`scripts/stage2.py`, `time_entry`/`persist_time_entry`, call sites at the attempt loops around the `started_wall, started_mono = utc_now(), monotonic_now()` lines) and writes the line accepted or rejected; `docs/WORKFLOW.md` §4 states an interactive session never writes a runner line and never logs an interval the runner is measuring.

**Where the verify-lock wait is already measured — but outside the plan.** `scripts/verify_lock.py` (module docstring) runs the machine-wide admission and measures the queue-plus-memory wait: `admission_id` and `admission_wait` go into the published ownership and into `HARNESS_VERIFY_ADMISSION_ID`/`HARNESS_VERIFY_ADMISSION_WAIT` in a protected child's environment; `wait_seconds = _now() - self.entered` is taken when the lock is admitted, and a nested entry inherits the wait as `reused`, never measured again (bead coding-harness-1um6, `admission_context()`). `scripts/verify_duration.py` (module docstring, "Time") keeps that wait out of execution seconds: `run_end.seconds` sums active gate segments so queue and memory waits stay outside, and a completion may carry the additive fields `admission_requested`, `admission_id`, `admission_wait_seconds`, `admission_reused` (bead coding-harness-bxxt.6). These records live in `verify-duration.jsonl` (full runs) and `verify-targeted.jsonl` (targeted checks, bead coding-harness-bxxt.7) under the shared state root, local only, outside the repository.

**The appetite comparison today.** `make report` (`scripts/run_report.py`, change block `# One change's block: attempts, calls, time against the card's appetite.`) sums the recorded seconds of the plan's time records and compares them against the card's `Appetite:` value, parsed by `appetite_seconds()` (positive hours and minutes compose, a competing second budget is refused) lifted by `card_appetite_value()` through the runner's own `CARD_LABELS`. It warns when `reading.seconds * 2 >= appetite` — "at or over half the appetite … advice on the snapshot, not a stop" — and treats unread evidence as `time not read` / `appetite not read` / `unread lines: N`, never as zero. `docs/WORKFLOW.md` §"The run report (`make report`)" states it is not a gate and starts, stops and locks nothing. README.md names the same figure ("The appetite: `make report CHANGE=<name>` shows the time used against it"). ADR 0025 point 4 (`docs/adr/0025-targeted-check-after-repair.md`) separately obliges the orchestrator to measure repair duration, verify-lock wait and full-check misses over five changes or two weeks and report to the maintainer.

## What is unclear about it

- **The 2026-11-05 decision is recorded but not implemented.** Not found: any rule, field, check or report in `prompts/`, `docs/WORKFLOW.md`, `scripts/` or `tests/` that excludes verify-lock waits from time used, or that reports an appetite overrun at the moment it happens (the run report warns at half appetite on a snapshot, against the raw sum). Not there is not the same as not found — this is the state of the files read, including `scripts/time_records.py` whole and `prompts/04-implement.md` whole.
- **Runner attempt seconds include the wait.** The runner's attempt span runs from before the model call to after the post-run check, so its recorded seconds cover any verify-admission wait inside the attempt; nothing splits it. The verify-duration history does separate the wait — but that history is local state, disconnected from the plan's `## Time used` and from `run_report.py`'s comparison. Whether runner lines should stay whole-attempt, or gain a split or a scope marker, is undecided by anything read.
- **"Working time" has no definition beyond spans.** Today's counted/uncounted boundary is only: break inside a session counts, time between sessions doesn't. The decision adds a third category (verify-lock waits don't count) with no named field in the `Record` grammar and no sentence in the appetite rule. Whether the interactive `time-log` route can even observe a wait it waited through (the session is blocked while `make verify` queues) is an open question this pass cannot answer from the repository.
- **The archived verify-duration research already flagged the contract:** whether a verify duration joins the `## Time used` grammar was left undecided because the grammar is deliberately single and describes a model attempt, not a gate run (`plans/changes/archive/2026-11-06-verify-duration/research.md`, question section). Any new field touches that settled single-grammar contract.
- **The bead's own notes are unread** (tracker database not read in this pass); the change description may carry the maintainer's wording of "working time" and "overrun".
