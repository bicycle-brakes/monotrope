## What exists today

The rule that a "journal citation gate" would mechanise already exists, at enforcement level 4: **RB-P5** in the rulebook (`docs/governance/regelwerk.md`, § P table): *"Der Verlauf ist append-only und wird nie ohne sein Datum zitiert. Ein Befundblock ist unveränderlich; Widerspruch kommt darunter, nie hinein"* — scope P L I, with two named enforcers: `tests/test_feedback_channel.py::test_handover_records_are_immutable` and `tests/test_provenance.py::test_journal_is_never_cited_without_a_date`. Level 4 per §3 means a rule whose enforcer must really run; a rule without an enforcer may sit at most at level 2 and must then name a `Ramp:` valve (Regelwerk frontmatter `rating_scheme` and §4 table notes).

The **date-citation half** of RB-P5 is enforced only as a plain pytest: `tests/test_provenance.py::test_journal_is_never_cited_without_a_date` (module docstring: "RB-P5: der Verlauf wird nie ohne sein Datum zitiert"). It runs `git ls-files '*.md'` (`_verfolgte_markdown()`) and flags every citation matching `_ZITAT` (`docs/journal/handover/<target>`) whose target is non-empty, does not start with `<` (convention placeholder), and carries no ISO date (`_ISO`, `\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}`). Its red-on-mutation proof is the sibling test `test_the_date_check_goes_red_when_a_citation_loses_its_date` (referring to finding F-A-28), which mutates on strings, not files. The module docstring claims "Das Ventil `journal-date-citation` im Regelwerk (§ P) benennt diesen Test" — that claim is not borne out today: a case-insensitive search for `journal-date-citation` over the whole worktree matches only this test file and the archived research of the provenance-location change; `docs/governance/regelwerk.md` names the test directly as RB-P5's enforcer and contains no `Ramp:journal-date-citation`.

## Which files a change would have to touch

Any gate addition lands in `decisions/gates.yaml` (human-decided → decision PR, title `decide(<scope>): …`), which forces regeneration of `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and of the `docs/governance/gate-catalog.md` inventory, plus a mutation test reaching the gate's script (with `tests/test_ci.py`'s ratchet constraints). If the one-script-per-gate pattern is followed, a new `scripts/check_*.py` beside the twelve existing ones. The existing pytest `tests/test_provenance.py` and `tests/test_feedback_channel.py` are the current enforcers RB-P5 names; a change that moves or narrows them touches the Regelwerk's RB-P5 row (human-decided, append-only changelog with its four mandatory lines). An exempting gate additionally touches `decisions/adoption.yaml`. `plans/INDEX.md` is machine-rendered and never hand-edited. The journal files themselves (`docs/journal/**`) are excluded from markdownlint but included in `test_provenance`'s scan; a citation-gate change need not edit any handover, since the measured Markdown stock is clean today.
