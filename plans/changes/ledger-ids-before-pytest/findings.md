| S-1 | MAJOR | SPEC | base | spec.md, criteria 6 and 7 | [AMBIGUITY] The spec never says what makes a missing ledger test "unrelated" to a repair. Reading A: a repair never fails the cheap check because of any missing ledger test. Each finding's proof is then guarded only by the existing check of its `fixed — tests/<path>::<function>` line (criterion 7), and the word "unrelated" adds nothing. Reading B: a missing ledger test counts as related, and fails the cheap check, when a `fixed —` line names it or when it belongs to the criterion the finding is about. Findings rows have no criterion cell, so the builder would have to invent that mapping. Under A, a repair whose `fixed —` line names a missing ledger test passes the cheap check and fails only later, at its red-first run. Under B it fails at the cheap check. Until one reading is chosen, the refutation of criterion 6 cannot be written as a test. | Opus | open | — |
| S-2 | MAJOR | SPEC | base | spec.md Terms "Ledger test" and criterion 6, against decisions.md Q-1 (2) | [AMBIGUITY] Q-1 lets a repair through only for "unwritten ledger tests", meaning entries still marked `not yet written`. The spec counts both entry forms as ledger tests and lets a repair through for any unrelated missing one. Take a repair that deletes or renames the test of a written entry (`- 3: tests/x.py::test_y`). Spec reading: test_y is named in the record, the cheap check does not fail, and the gap shows up only when a later gate runs (the criterion-ledger gate reports `"test_y" is not defined`). Decision reading: only entries marked `not yet written` are let through, so a missing test of a written entry fails the repair, as it does today. | Opus | open | — |
| S-3 | MAJOR | SPEC | base | spec.md Terms "Attempt record" and criterion 2 | The spec defines the attempt record only as "the runner's persistent record" and does not say where it is. Today an attempt can leave traces in several places: the `## Time used` line in plan.md, the checkpoint commit message, the failed attempt's diagnostics block in findings.md, and the loop record outside the worktree. For a passing attempt the runner deliberately writes its scope only to the time line and the commit message, and nothing after its audit line in findings.md (`scripts/stage2.py:10075-10077`, `:10673-10675`). So for a successful repair (criterion 2) the builder has to choose where the missing ids go. Until the place is fixed, the refutation "a missing ID is absent from the record" has nowhere to look, and a test can pass by checking any one of these places. | Opus | open | — |
| S-4 | MAJOR | SPEC | base | spec.md criterion 9 | The phase-6 refusal has to name "the next step", but the spec never says what that step is. Criterion 5 together with the runner's marker clearing removes every marker by the time implement is accepted. So this refusal only fires after a plan has been redrafted, and under criterion 6 repairs no longer owe those tests. The spec names no verb that does owe them: rerunning implement, a repair of some taken row, or writing the test by hand and committing the marker's removal. It also leaves open the case where an entry is still marked but its test now exists, for example written by hand with no runner attempt since to clear the marker: does phase 6 refuse on the marker text or treat the entry as clear? Because the correct step is never stated, any sentence satisfies "the refusal omits how to proceed", so that part of criterion 9 cannot go red. | Opus | open | — |
| S-5 | MAJOR | SPEC | base | spec.md criteria 6 and Definition of Done; `scripts/stage2.py:6786-6812` | Implement and repair share `MUST_RUN_DELEGATED` and `MUST_RUN_COMMANDS`. Both prompts tell the model to write every ledger test, including rows marked `not yet written`, and `MUST_RUN_DELEGATED` gives the reason "a missing ledger test fails the attempt". `MUST_RUN_COMMANDS` also has Codex run pytest over the full ledger list, which includes ids that are missing. Once criterion 6 holds, both statements are false for repair. Neither the criteria nor the Definition of Done (which names only `docs/WORKFLOW.md`) says whether the repair prompts change. Left as they are, repairs are still told to write tests unrelated to their findings, which is the burden decisions.md Q-1 describes from the incident, and Codex's own must-run pytest still hits the missing ids. | Opus | open | — |
| S-6 | MINOR | SPEC | base | spec.md Terms "Standing" and Open decisions [DETAIL] on existence | [AMBIGUITY] The DETAIL says "a readable file and a top-level function, matching the ledger's existing test form", but the repository already has two existence checks that disagree. The gate and `clear_written` match the text `^def <function>\(` (`scripts/check_criterion_ledger.py:303-304`). That misses `async def` and accepts a `def` at column 0 inside a string. That conflicts with criterion 11, whose "unparseable" implies the pre-check parses the file. | Opus | open | — |
