## What exists today

**The bead and its intent.** The change is bead `coding-harness-k93f`, described in the night-run plan as "the runner writes the fixed-line skeleton per taken row; the model fills only the test or the reason", class `bounded`, lane A (the runner), queue item 3 (`docs/research/2026-10-28-night-run-plan.md`, "The three queues", lane A item 3). The handover of that night names it P1, queue 3, "then package 4 (lead coding-harness-6jg)" and gives the lessons that produced it (`docs/journal/handover/2026-10-28-three-lane-night.md`, "Open work and blockers" and "Lessons the orchestrator applies from now on"): a failed attempt's edits become the repair baseline; a row whose evidence is a measurement or text is taken as `not a test` with the orchestrator recording the evidence; "every taken row needs a fixed line and a `Broke a test` mark before the next round". `plans/INDEX.md` registers the change folder as draft with no implementation (line 273). The change folder holds only `research.md` — no `decisions.md`, `spec.md` or `plan.md` exists yet; its frontmatter carries no `class:` field (the class comes from the planning documents and the `--class` argument, per ADR 0020's routing).

**The repair verb and its answer contract.** `python3 scripts/stage2.py repair <change>` (phase 4b and 6) collects every taken row of the loop record, every released block and every phase-6 table (`repair_targets`, `scripts/stage2.py:2811`), refuses while a row is undecided, an id doubled, or a Status still begins `taken` while naming a fix in the legacy forms `; fixed by`, `; fixed:`, `; repaired by` (`LEGACY_FIXED`, `scripts/stage2.py:2842`; refusal in `repair`, `scripts/stage2.py:7712`), and hands all taken rows to one guarded call. The route ladder is Codex (`gpt-6.1-sol`), a fresh Codex attempt with the first one's diagnostics, then Sonnet 5.5 as `stage-1`; `--stage2-off` runs Sonnet alone (`repair`, `scripts/stage2.py:7728`; ADR 0017, `docs/adr/0017-codex-repairs-taken-findings.md`). No GLM model may appear in the routes — a test plants that fault explicitly (`tests/test_stage2.py:9037`).

The model must itself write the whole **fixed line** per taken row: `<ID>: fixed — tests/<path>::<function>` or `<ID>: fixed — not a test — <reason>`. This contract lives in `REPAIR_ANSWER` (`scripts/stage2.py:274`), embedded in `REPAIR_FORMAT` (Codex, may run tests and `make verify`) and `REPAIR_FORMAT_FILE_TOOLS` (the file-tools fallback, `scripts/stage2.py:296–305`). The prompt lists the taken rows as `- <ID> [<Severity>, <Dimension>] <Where>: <Finding> — Status: <Status>` (`repair_prompt`, `scripts/stage2.py:7667`, section `## Taken rows` at line 7680) — no skeleton lines, no fill-in template. A grep for `skeleton`/`fill in` over `scripts/stage2.py` finds nothing: **not there** — today no runner-written per-row skeleton or fill-in blank exists anywhere in the runner's prompts. The closest relative is the loop's mark round, `MARK_FORMAT` (`scripts/stage2.py:273`), which also asks the model to compose whole lines (`<ID>: agree` / `<ID>: disagree — <reason>`), and the asked-again `FORMAT_REMINDER` after a malformed answer, which is wired only for review-loop members (`scripts/stage2.py:8070`), **not found** for the repair verb — a repair retry instead receives the previous attempt's diagnostics quoted into the prompt (`repair_prompt`, `scripts/stage2.py:7684`).

**How the answer is checked.** `check_repair_answer` (`scripts/stage2.py:6043`) collects per taken row the lines whose right side starts `fixed` followed by a dash, ignoring other prose (`scripts/stage2.py:6108`); zero claims fail as `no fixed line`, two distinct claims as `several fixed lines` (`scripts/stage2.py:6109–6117`); `fixed_status` (`scripts/stage2.py:5999`) validates the body against `FIXED_TEST` (`^tests/…::test_\w+`, `scripts/stage2.py:427`, "in the form of ADR 0013's criterion ledger") or the `not a test — <reason>` form, refusing `.`/`..` path segments. Only then do the red-first proof (the named test must fail on the baseline's code) and the fixed-code proof run, and the runner writes each row's Status via `mark_repaired_findings` (`scripts/stage2.py:6199`) and the loop record's rows via `mark_repaired_record` (`scripts/stage2.py:6228`, the record lives outside the repository under `${XDG_STATE_HOME:-~/.local/state}/coding-harness/stage2/<change>@<digest>/loop.md`, `scripts/stage2.py:122` and `read_record` at `scripts/stage2.py:3056`). `Broke a test` marks are derived from the proof evidence, not asked from the model (`scripts/stage2.py:6162–6177`; doctrine "`Broke a test` stays the orchestrator's mark", `docs/WORKFLOW.md` §4, "Repairing taken findings (repair)", around line 1548). Every attempt's complete answer is retained as protected local evidence under the worktree's git directory, `stage2/attempts/<change>/attempt-<n>-<token>.md`, named in the attempt's diagnostics (`docs/WORKFLOW.md` §4, same section, around lines 1539–1545).

**The failure mode the bead answers.** Format rejections of exactly this shape are frequent in real findings files: `plans/changes/run-report/findings.md:181–243` shows the 2026-10-29 repair of `run-report` burning the whole ladder — attempt 1 (codex) ten rows `no fixed line`, attempt 2 (codex-repair) `L1-6: no fixed line`, attempt 3 (stage-1) a fixed line that then failed the red-first proof — before the loop took the rows over with hand-recorded evidence. The same diagnostic stands in `plans/changes/pi-pin/findings.md:616` (F-1 through F-14), `plans/changes/venv-pycache/findings.md:71`, `plans/changes/swarm-test-speed/findings.md:228`, `plans/changes/review-loop-deep-a/findings.md:258` (`several fixed lines`) and `plans/changes/ratchet-mutation-evidence/findings.md:132`. The spec of `repair-fixed-line-format` records four rejected Sonnet repair attempts on 2026-10-26 whose answers were not kept (`plans/changes/repair-fixed-line-format/spec.md`, "Problem"). On the loop side, the same mechanism family is documented: the red-first lane got stuck on glm-5.3-flash answering in the wrong format, and the maintainer declined to retire the model — "format is the runner's job" became bead `coding-harness-np8z`, architectural (`docs/journal/handover/2026-10-28-three-lane-night.md`, "Decisions and preferences"). This change is the bounded step of that direction for the repair verb.
