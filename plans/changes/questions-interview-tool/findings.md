| F-1 | BLOCKER | SPEC | base | spec.md AC 2 and AC 6; Terms "Native question tool" | [AMBIGUITY] It is unclear how a plan card goes through a tool call that waits for an answer. AC 2 puts plan cards under "exactly one question per tool call". `prompts/03-plan.md` says phase 4 starts once the card is shown and no "ok" is needed, and AC 6 is refuted if an in-scope card needs affirmative approval. A native tool call such as AskUserQuestion returns only when the maintainer picks an option. Reading A: the card goes verbatim into the tool call, with options the spec does not name, and the session waits. Every card then needs an answer before phase 4 starts, which is AC 6's refutation in practice. Reading B: the card is shown as message text, and AC 2's "including plan cards" means one card per message, not per call. The two readings give different `03-plan.md` text and different AC 2 and AC 6 tests. | Opus | resolved 2026-11-03 by the maintainer (15:00 block, interview tool): A — plan card as message text, one per message; veto stays; tool only for questions needing an answer (maintainer 2026-11-03 15:0x) | — |

| F-3 | MAJOR | SPEC | base | spec.md AC 4 "When the maintainer is away" | [AMBIGUITY] "Away" is undefined, and the workflow already collects questions by day. `docs/WORKFLOW.md` collects all non-urgent questions for the 15:00 block even while the maintainer is present.
