**What exists today.**
- The loop has no round cap. It ends on a clean round, when the paid ladder is spent, or on the repair brake. Source: `docs/adr/0021-review-swarms-until-clean.md`, decision "End on a review with no accepted findings rather than a fixed round count"; `scripts/stage2.py`, comment above `STATE_KEY` (around line 423), "never on a round count (ADR 0021)".
- The shape of rounds is in `docs/WORKFLOW.md` §4b (table of review stages, around lines 105–109): round 1 is the free swarm, round 2 the Flash swarm of up to three paid models in parallel, then one paid model per round, cheapest first by declared `anfragen_5h`. The model set and cost values are in `model-endpoints.yaml`, which I did not read.
- The brake is in `docs/WORKFLOW.md`, around lines 1512–1514, and `docs/how-it-works.md`, lines 129–131: two consecutive rounds whose only accepted findings begin `fix-r` stop the loop for the maintainer. Earlier text (`docs/WORKFLOW.md`, lines 162–167) gives the reason: every defect of rounds two to four came from a rebuild.
- A round over a diff larger than the review bound is marked cut, and a cut round is never clean. `scripts/stage2.py`, `REVIEW_DIFF_MAX_LINES = 2000` (around line 450); the comment at around line 2562 ("a clean answer over a cut diff is no clean round"); `CUT_ROUNDS_LINE` and `ROUNDS_LINE` (lines 462–463).
- Repairs happen between rounds, each followed by a targeted check; the full `make verify` runs before phase 6. `docs/WORKFLOW.md`, "Repairing taken findings", from around line 1545.
