## What exists today

**The change and its decision.** `plans/changes/doc-currency/decisions.md` holds the class (`bounded`, overriding the bead's proposed `feature`) and the maintainer's answer D of 2026-11-22: a small script (named there as "for example `scripts/doc_currency.py`") reads the paths changed on main since the last handover, maps areas to documents through a fixed mapping, writes `possibly stale: <doc> — new since the last handover: <change>` lines into the handover, opens one bead per possibly stale document (none when one is open already), is called by the handover routine itself, never blocks and never edits documents. The research file `plans/changes/doc-currency/research.md` is still the empty skeleton with a stage-2 call line (explore, attempt 1, route flash, model glm-5.3-flash, leak check clean). The bead is `coding-harness-ni8`; `plans/INDEX.md` already carries rows for both artefacts (dated 2026-11-23).

**The handover routine — the caller the decision names.** The routine is the `/handover` skill at `tools/claude-code/skills/handover/SKILL.md`: it writes `docs/journal/handover/<date>-<topic>.md` with a fixed section order (`# Resume here`, `## Where we are`, `## Next step`, `## Needed from the maintainer`, `## Decisions and preferences`, `## Open work and blockers`, `## Working state and parallel-session boundary`, `## Validation`), takes its frontmatter field list from `scripts/handover_schema.py` (falling back to variant A of `templates/handover.md`), always opens a pull request, and states as a rule that it makes no tracker writes while another session runs in the same repository ("Keine Schreibvorgänge im Tracker …", SKILL.md section 4). It is installed into `~/.claude/` by `tools/claude-code/install.sh`; AGENTS.md ("The maintainer's Claude Code display") requires changes to happen in `tools/claude-code/` and a reinstall, never only in `~/.claude`. The supporting hook `tools/claude-code/hooks/handover.sh` implements `where`/`show`/`start`/`note`; its `list_handovers` orders handover files by the date in the file name, then mtime, and its `next_step()` reads the last "next step"-like heading. `docs/WORKFLOW.md` ("The eighth movement: the session handover") defines the artefact and its gate: frontmatter against `scripts/handover_schema.py`, direction/provenance/goal-standing required; `templates/handover.md` documents `target_ref` as "the commit being reported on", and a count grep found `^target_ref:` in all 79 handover files under `docs/journal/handover/` — so a git anchor for "since the last handover" exists in every file. `tests/test_feedback_channel.py` checks template against schema (named by both the skill's section 5 and the template's header). `scripts/outcome_test.py` (`TARGETS = {"AGENTS.md", "handover-convention"}`, and the `handovers` resolver reading the session-handover section of `docs/WORKFLOW.md`) implements the rule in `docs/WORKFLOW.md` (the outcome-test section): before every change to `AGENTS.md` or the handover convention, a pre-change measurement with `--purpose pre-change --target handover-convention` is run. Editing the skill or the eighth movement is such an edit.

**The mapping targets and their current state.** All documents the decision names exist: `README.md` documents the make targets in its "Getting started" section (`make venv`, `make verify`, `make change NAME=… BEAD=…`); `docs/how-it-works.md` carries sections on the phases, who checks what, the review and research swarms, runner details, classes, design rules and testing; `docs/architecture/` holds four files including the arc42-style `docs/architecture/target-architecture.md` (quality goals, constraints, building blocks, runtime views); ADRs live in `docs/adr/` as `0001-…` through `0025-…` (25 files); `ROADMAP.md` and `AGENTS.md` sit at the root. `provenance.yaml` fixes who may write what: `docs/adr/` and `decisions/gates.yaml` are `allowed: [human-decided]` (a decision pull request), `docs/architecture/` and `docs/research/` are `ai-drafted-reviewed` with frontmatter required, `docs/journal/handover/` allows `ai-generated`/`ai-drafted-reviewed` with frontmatter required, `templates/` is `human-written` with `frontmatter: none`, `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` are `human-written`, and `plans/INDEX.md` is `machine-rendered`. The `docs/architecture/` originals are additionally protected by dated amendment banners and decision-continuity tests, as the accepted change `plans/changes/archive/2026-11-06-architecture-reconcile/spec.md` records; its remedy was a dated comparison report under `docs/research/`, not edits to the documents.

**What checks currency today — and what does not.** Three mechanisms exist, none of them covering the decision's scope. `scripts/check_research_currency.py` lists notes under `docs/research/` whose `last_reviewed:` is more than 90 days old; it is report-only (exit 0 over any content, exit 3 when nothing was measured), reads no git, network or tracker, and is wired as the gate `research-currency` in `decisions/gates.yaml` (entry `id: research-currency`, `job: drift`, `enforcement: report`, `floor: false`, `placement: both`, with the maintainer's 2026-10-05 reason) — the generated `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs it in the `drift` job with `continue-on-error: true`; the file is GENERATED by `python3 scripts/ci_gates.py --generate-ci` and hand-edits fail the `ci-drift` gate (header of `.github/workflows/ci.yml`). `scripts/plans_registry.py --check` (Makefile target `staleness`) holds the age rule for `plans/` only. `scripts/check_docs.py` checks structure only — required frontmatter keys, dead links, dead anchors — in German identifiers (`PFlicht`, `_anker`, `befunde`; German in `scripts/` is translated in pull requests of its own per AGENTS.md house rule 1, bead `coding-harness-yim`). That no check compares `docs/architecture/`, `docs/how-it-works.md` or `README.md` against recently changed code is the "not there" statement: it is not found in any script under `scripts/` (the full listing was read), and `decisions.md` states it as the motivation ("today nothing does"). The closest sibling change, `plans/changes/research-currency/spec.md` (active, class `bounded`, bead `coding-harness-pu9`, `implementation_status: partial` per its header and the INDEX rows), explicitly excluded exactly this scope as non-goals: "Other folders — `docs/architecture/`, `docs/adr/`, `docs/governance/`, the handovers".

**Precedents the mechanism would sit on.** `scripts/touch_map.py` computes changed files between two refs from git objects only (`rev-parse`, `merge-base`, `diff-tree`), deterministically, with a written spec at `plans/changes/archive/2026-10-06-touch-map/spec.md` (path named in its docstring; the spec itself I have not read). On the tracker side, `scripts/close_merged_beads.py` is the one existing script that writes the tracker: it closes beads through the pinned `tools/bd` as argv calls, reads state only through `bead_render.render_pfad()` — the one access point declared in `scripts/bead_render.py` (bead `1jg.3`, ADR 0004) — validates the render with `check_bead_export.run`, refuses a preset `BEAD_RENDER` for write runs, and ends with `tools/bd dolt push`. A grep for bead creation across `scripts/` found no matches: **no script creates beads today**, so answer D's "opens one bead per possibly stale document" has no script-side precedent, only the session-side `tools/bd create` (AGENTS.md tracker quick reference). A read-side precedent for listing beads programmatically is `tools/claude-code/hooks/handover.sh` (`beads_json`, calling `tools/bd list --status in_progress … --json`).

**The ratchets a new script runs into.** `tests/test_ci.py` demands that every script under `scripts/` has a caller: either a step in the generated `ci.yml` or a named entry with a reason in `ci_gates.OHNE_AUFRUFER` (imported from `scripts/ci_gates.py`; the tests refuse reasons shorter than ten characters), plus the `OHNE_MUTATIONSTEST` ratchet asking for mutation evidence; the research-currency spec (criterion 13) names the same four lists (`OHNE_AUFRUFER`, `OHNE_TEST`, `OHNE_MUTATIONSTEST`, kit `REIST_NICHT`) as the ones that must not grow. `scripts/kit/manifest.yaml` classifies every file by longest matching prefix into `mechanismus`/`bestand`, and an unclassified file is a BLOCK, not a default include. Job prompts (`prompts/`) are bound by `docs/WORKFLOW.md` ("Recurring jobs"): they "write no tracker and take no git action of their own" — a boundary relevant if the mechanism were ever shaped as a job prompt, which the decision does not do.

## What is unclear about it

- **Placement of the "possibly stale" lines.** The decision says the lines go "into the handover"; the skill's section order is fixed (eight sections, SKILL.md section 3) and neither the skill, the template, nor the eighth movement names a section for them. Not found in any source; open.
- **The fixed mapping.** Only examples exist (`decisions.md`: `scripts/verify_*` and Makefile → `docs/how-it-works.md`; new ADRs → `docs/architecture/`; new make targets → `README.md`). No mapping table exists anywhere in the repository (not found); it would live in the new script.
- **What "the last handover" anchors to.** Every handover carries `target_ref:` (79 of 79), and `list_handovers` in `hooks/handover.sh` orders by filename date plus mtime; same-day multiple handovers and addenda exist (e.g. 2026-10-15 has two files). Whether the script reads the newest file's `target_ref`, its filename date, or something else is stated nowhere; open.
- **Bead creation from a script.** No precedent exists (`bd create` appears in no script). The dedupe rule "none when one is open already" needs a recognition convention (title shape) that no source defines, and the read path (`bead_render.render_pfad()` vs `tools/bd list --json`) is undecided. There is also an unresolved tension to surface, not to resolve here: house rule 4 (AGENTS.md) and the skill's own rule 4 restrict tracker writes to the first-tracker-writer session while a second writing session runs, and the handover routine runs precisely at session end. `decisions.md` does not address it.
- **Wire-in beyond the handover.** Whether the script also runs in the nightly `drift` job as a `report` gate (the research-currency shape, requiring a `human-decided` entry in `decisions/gates.yaml` plus regeneration of `ci.yml`) or stays handover-only (then needing an `OHNE_AUFRUFER` entry with a reason in `scripts/ci_gates.py` to satisfy `tests/test_ci.py`) is not stated in `decisions.md`; open.
- **The vault.** The bead's night-run description reads "The handover checks the architecture docs, the front page and the vault" (`docs/journal/handover/2026-10-15-night-run-fourth.md`), but answer D names only repository documents. Whether the vault is in scope is not stated; open.
- **Not read here:** `plans/specs/implementation-verification/spec.md` (the only living spec), the `touch-map` and `tracker-access-point` archived specs, and the remaining ~28 sections of the `architecture-reconcile` spec — their neighbourhood is possible but unverified in this pass.
