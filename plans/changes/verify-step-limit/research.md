**The runner's step limits exist as three hard-coded constants.** `scripts/stage2.py` defines `TOOL_SECONDS = 5 * 60`, `VERIFY_SECONDS = 30 * 60` and `GIT_SECONDS = 2 * 60` (module constants, `scripts/stage2.py:4329-4331`), plus the drain bound `KILL_DRAIN_SECONDS = 5` (`:917`). Around them sit the other named bounds: `REVIEWER_MINUTES = 45` (`:266`, foreign loop calls), `RED_FIRST_MINUTES = 10` (`:509`), `PI_DISCOVERY_SECONDS = 120` (`:440`), `PI_VERSION_SECONDS = 30` (`:270`). The timeout mechanism is `launch()` (`scripts/stage2.py:974`): with a timeout the child leads its own process group, `communicate(timeout=...)` bounds it, a timeout kills the whole group and raises `StepTimedOut` ("a started program ran into its time limit and its whole process group was killed", R-20, class at `:646-647`) with the message "`{command[0]}` was stopped by {limit}" (`:1055-1057`); groups are registered in `_LIVE_GROUPS` so an interrupted runner kills them (`kill_live`, `:920`). There is **no configuration surface for the seconds constants**: a search over `scripts/stage2.py` for environment overrides found only `STAGE2_RED_REPORT`/`STAGE2_RED_TOKEN`/`STAGE2_RED_SELECTOR` (`:529-531`), and no command-line argument sets a step timeout — the limits are code, not configuration (contrast the verify admission's memory floor, which `HARNESS_VERIFY_MEMORY_FLOOR` does configure).

**`VERIFY_SECONDS` bounds exactly three verify-shaped steps.** (1) `verify_attempt()` (`scripts/stage2.py:6297`) — an implement or repair attempt's post-run check: format, cheap checks, ledger clearing, derived preparation, then `make verify` under one machine admission with `timeout=VERIFY_SECONDS` (`:6412-6419`); a timeout becomes the diagnostic "`make verify` did not finish: …" (`:6425`). (2) `full_verify()` (`:6179`) — the full check of a repair whose targeted coverage was not safe, and phase 6's boundary, same admission and timeout (`:6194-6201`, diagnostic at `:6205`). (3) the draft verb's document check `DOCS_VERIFY = ["bash", "scripts/verify_all.sh", "--job", "docs"]` (`:9266-9267`), run under its own admission with the same `VERIFY_SECONDS` (`:9308-9316`), reporting "`bash scripts/verify_all.sh --job docs` could not run: …" on failure to start (`:9320`). Everything smaller is bounded more tightly: the targeted mode runs the selected pytest, ruff and per-gate commands under `TOOL_SECONDS` (`:6150`, `:6164`; the cheap checks in `cheap_checks()` at `:5807` are ruff lint, ruff format and pytest over selected tests), and every `git` call runs under `GIT_SECONDS` (`:2502`, `:4195`, `:4531`, `:5339`, `:6588`, `:9183`).

**The step limit is pinned by tests, not prose.** `tests/test_stage2.py` holds: the admission-wait exclusion — a queued check must survive a 2-second `VERIFY_SECONDS` with a real second of queueing, "the admission wait consumed the step limit" as the planted fault (`:2201-2223`, criterion 4 of the machine lock); the call-site pin that `make verify` keeps the shared limit ("the shared step limit was swapped", `:3958-3985`) and that a hung check ends through the real launch (`:4031-4033`); the literal message pin "the runner's step limit after 30 minutes" (`:6418`, `:24877`); the O188-1/R-20 distribution pin — renderer and ruff get `TOOL_SECONDS`, `make` gets `VERIFY_SECONDS` (`:6444-6471`); the make timeout equality (`:24536`) and git's `GIT_SECONDS` (`:25093`). `tests/test_stage2_fail_fast.py` monkeypatches `TOOL_SECONDS` to 0.5 for its fail-fast cases (`:419`, `:468`). **Not found:** any mention of `VERIFY_SECONDS`, `TOOL_SECONDS` or `GIT_SECONDS` in `docs/` — the constants are documented nowhere outside code and tests (grep over `docs/` returned no matches). The doctrine that exists names the mechanism only in effect: `docs/WORKFLOW.md` (admission section) states "The queue and memory waits consume no runner step limit and no pytest proof's execution timeout; the configured limits still bind the executing check" (§ around line 580), and `docs/orchestration.md`'s "Stated, enforced, planned" table marks the row "Runner step limit, leak check, red first" as *enforced by the runner* (`docs/orchestration.md:134`).

**The admission lock moved the wait out of the limit — deliberately.** `scripts/verify_lock.py` holds the machine-wide admission (one verify-bearing step at a time, memory floor 25 percent default) and states its **Boundary**: "the admission protects the machine's load, not data … that overlap is an accepted limit, bounded by the runner's step limits (spec, 'Boundary')" (`scripts/verify_lock.py:44-49`). Its archived change decided explicitly: "**First come, first served; no priority for runner attempts.** The bead's note of 2026-10-28 gave a runner attempt's verify priority, because the step limit could kill it while it waited. This change takes the wait out of the step limit, so priority is no longer needed for correctness" (`plans/changes/archive/2026-11-06-verify-machine-lock/decisions.md:78-83`, bead coding-harness-p6mu). The run report and duration helper keep admission wait *reported beside*, never inside, execution seconds (`scripts/verify_all.sh:290-297`; `scripts/verify_duration.py`).

**The limit has a recorded incident history — the margin is measured thin.** (1) Origin: runner review R-20 (2026-10-20) found the runner's own steps unbounded and proposed "a generous bound (e.g. 60 minutes) with a **named** abort — never a silent cut" (`docs/journal/handover/2026-10-20-review-swarm-runner-review.md:473-486`); what landed was 30 minutes for verify — tighter than proposed. (2) The three-lane night trial: three parallel full `make verify` runs on the 8-core machine drove load above 100 and past the 30-minute limit; one at a time, a run took 11–12 minutes (`docs/journal/handover/2026-10-28-three-lane-night.md:32-34`; same events behind `docs/orchestration.md:39-44`). (3) `red-first-pytest-failed` lost multiple attempts to the limit — findings record "`make verify` could not run: make was stopped by the runner's step limit after 30 minutes" and the "did not finish" variant across attempts 2–3 (`plans/changes/archive/2026-11-06-red-first-pytest-failed/findings.md:101`, `:182`, `:367`; spec background `:41-45`). (4) The interrupted night of 2026-11-10: "A second full check beside the lock holder pushed both past the runner's 30-minute limit; one green run alone takes 26 to 28 minutes, so the margin is thin (epic coding-harness-bxxt)" (`docs/journal/handover/2026-11-10-night-run-interrupted.md:75-77`). (5) The twelve-lane night of 2026-11-10/11: "the runner's 30-minute step limit each cost a change its attempts on 2026-11-10; none was a fault of the code under test" (`docs/journal/handover/2026-11-11-night-run-twelve-lanes.md:204-206`), in a night where verify-slot waits grew from 35 to 147 minutes and a green full check measured 1515 s of execution (`:174-185`, `:191-193`). (6) A repair of a change that touches `scripts/stage2.py` runs the full check, not a targeted one — so runner changes always pay the full 26–28-minute check against the 30-minute bound (`docs/journal/handover/2026-11-11-night-run-twelve-lanes.md:194-195`).
