## Rules for this run

1. **Night until 07:00 UTC, day from 07:00 to 11:00 UTC.** Night rules from the
   previous plan hold, with one change: a pull request without a decision gets
   `gh pr merge --auto --merge --match-head-commit <sha>` when it leaves draft,
   at night too. The maintainer decided this on 2026-10-31 (bead
   coding-harness-p3xq). Decision pull requests wait for the maintainer's word.
   Never `--admin`.
2. **Where a change stops:**
   - A change whose interview is complete runs up to its plan card.
   - Implementation starts only on the maintainer's word. The exception is
     coding-harness-l1if, whose plan card the maintainer approved on
     2026-10-31.
   - A change that touches `model-endpoints.yaml`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/adr/` or
     `.github/workflows/` stops before that file. Its text is proposed to the
     maintainer.
3. **Hot files:** `scripts/stage2.py` is written by one lane at a time. Lane A
   holds it until coding-harness-l1if has its pull request, then hands it to
   lane B.
4. **Checks:** a hand repair gets a targeted check. A full `make verify` runs
   before phase 6 and before a pull request leaves draft. A `prompts/` change
   owes a full verify.
5. **Appetite:** each change carries the appetite on its plan card. The
   appetite extends once by itself, up to twice its size (PR #292), and the
   extension is recorded in the plan's time section.
6. **Questions:** they collect in the handover for the next 15:00 block. At
   most one goes to the chat, and only when a lane is blocked without it.
7. **Status:** a short status in the chat at 07:00 UTC. The handover goes as a
   pull request from its own worktree by 10:45 UTC.
