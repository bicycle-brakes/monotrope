# Commercial licensing

Monotrope is source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0
([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). It is not an OSI-approved open-source license.
Noncommercial use is free. Commercial use needs a separate written license from
the copyright holder.

## Why this license

The repository was public under Apache 2.0, which allowed commercial use,
including closed-source products. The maintainer wants noncommercial use and
collaboration to stay free while commercial use needs an agreement. This
replaces the Apache-2.0 choice in the public-readiness decision D-17
(`docs/architecture/target-architecture.md`) for future releases; the rest of
that decision stands.

## What needs a commercial license

Use in a business, a paid product or a paid service; use primarily intended
for commercial advantage or monetary compensation; hosting Monotrope for
customers; or embedding it in a commercial offering. When in doubt, ask before
you rely on it.

This page is a summary. The license text controls, and nothing on this page
grants a commercial license by itself.

## How to get one

Contact Michael Peichl at <https://github.com/MPAdesso>. Say:

- who you are and which legal entity will use Monotrope;
- what you want to do: internal use, a product, a hosted service, or
  redistribution;
- how many people or systems will use it;
- whether you will make it available to third parties.

A commercial license exists only after a separate written agreement is signed.
It can be limited in time, scope, users, or deployment.

## Contributions

Contributions are welcome. Before a contribution is merged, the contributor
must agree that:

1. they wrote the contribution or have the right to submit it;
2. it may be distributed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0; and
3. the maintainer may also license it under a commercial license.

If you cannot agree to all three, contact the maintainer before opening a pull
request. A formal contributor license agreement can replace this statement for
larger contributions.

## Earlier releases

Releases published before 2026-10-20 were under Apache 2.0. That grant remains
for those releases and cannot be withdrawn. This repository and future
releases are under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
